Growth reading (D-04, OFFC-06, recorded for the orchestrator to confirm at phase verification): 'rooms and clusters grow with agent count' is delivered as follows. The building grows by columns as projects are added (07-02 buildBuilding). A cluster grows with its drawn agents: its ClusterAnchor rect is the padded bounding box of its seats, so it covers more desks and floor as agents arrive, and the sign follows its top-left. The project room keeps the fixed 16x12-tile size of the ported habblaud room (07-02), so room capacity is bounded by the caps of 07-06 and anything above them is the exact '+N agents' group. Making the room tile grid itself larger per agent would change 07-02's layout and is not planned.

Choices made here under the planner's discretion, each testable: (1) the manifest gives this plan no limits file, so the scene constants (culling margin, z-order bands, label sizes, beat counts, walker cap) live in a named block at the top of `camera.ts` and the wave caps at the top of `wave-trigger.ts`; (2) `camera.ts` holds all pure scene logic (zoom, placement, camera, culling, keys, life director) because the frozen ownership list has no other pure file, and the components stay thin; (3) the room theme variant of a project is the FNV-1a hash of its id, so a project keeps its look when rooms reorder; (4) the day position drives only the two nearest tint variants of each visible layer, so the first load is bounded by the measured scene, and characters are not tinted; (5) the life director and the day clock run on the beat of the shared clock bank (tier 3 changes every 800 ms) through an Animated value listener, so no new timer exists; (6) a desk screen, board and window are baked static variants and show no per-agent state.
