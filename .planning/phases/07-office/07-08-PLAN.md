<objective>
Wave 3 of Phase 7: motion and life for the office characters (OFFC-09, OFFC-12, OFFC-13). Build the character component that shows one server-rendered sprite sheet as a clipped sheet window and steps its frames on shared clocks, the walk planner that turns `findPath` tile steps into timed straight segments, the journeys built from it (handoff walk to the parent and back, elevator arrival and departure, outings), the runner that executes a journey against injected animation primitives, and the pure idle life of the common areas: a spot ledger, weighted idle wandering and group hangouts ported from habblaud's rules.

Purpose: 07-11 composes these into the building scene. Characters must move by transform from a bounded set of shared clocks (D-01, OFFC-09), a child must walk to its parent along the room grid and back and must not walk when no parent is reachable (D-04, OFFC-12), and idle agents must wander and meet in the lobby, kitchen, lounge and hallway only after the spike recorded a pass (D-06, D-12, OFFC-13). Every decision is a pure function of its inputs (map, reservations, rng, time), so it is tested offline without a host; only `character.tsx` and `clocks.ts` touch react and react-native, and they reuse the sheet-window and shared-timer technique that 07-03 measured on the host.

Output: 11 source files (`client/limits-life.ts`, `client/clocks.ts`, `client/character.tsx`, and `client/life/{frames,walk,journey,bank,runner,ledger,wander,social}.ts`), `tests/ui/office-life.test.mjs` and `.planning/phases/07-office/07-08-SUMMARY.md`.
