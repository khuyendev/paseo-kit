<objective>
Close verification gap 3 (SC4 and SC7, OFFC-11, OFFC-12, OFFC-13): three medium findings of plan 07-11 were reviewed, deferred and are still open on HEAD. The first is confirmed by reading camera.ts: `finished()` hides any finished departure without looking at the current status, and the reopen branch of `step()` only schedules an arrival for an entry that is already hidden, so an agent that reopens during its departure walk is hidden with no arrival due and is never drawn again until reload. The second is that office-screen.tsx never calls `store.retry`, so one transient `office.sprites` failure blanks a sprite key for the plugin lifetime. The third is that the life director is rebuilt with every new building, dropping the hidden, arrival and sleep state of every agent.

Purpose: remove three ways for a character or a layer to vanish without a cause the owner can see, with a test that fails first for each.

Output: edits to `plugins/office/client/camera.ts` (the departure return, `relayout`, the retry rule), `plugins/office/client/office-screen.tsx` (one director per mount, the retry on a slow beat) and new tests in `tests/ui/office-scene.test.mjs`.

Decisions honoured: D-04 (the scene is a function of the model and the layout), D-06 (life decisions are functions of inputs and the beat count, no random or clock in the pure module), D-09 (read-only), D-01 and OFFC-08 (every sprite key asked once, a transient failure may be asked again; the envelope and codes of 07-01 stay frozen), OFFC-11 and OFFC-12 (leave and arrive by elevator, handoff walks).

Gap-closure root cause (single why round): an execution miss. 07-11's plan named the life director's state, the arrival schedule and the sprite store's `retry`, but its tests covered the straight cases (close then leave, initialise then arrive, one failure then settled) and not the crossings of two transitions. The fix targets the crossings, not the straight paths.
